🧠 Explainable AI Dashboard

Explore how the Random Forest model identifies phishing websites using three Explainable AI (XAI) techniques: SHAP, LIME, and Feature Importance. These visualizations help interpret the model's decision-making process and highlight the factors that most influence predictions.

SHAP Summary Plot

The SHAP Summary Plot shows the overall impact of each feature on the model's predictions across the dataset. Features at the top contribute more significantly to phishing detection than those at the bottom.


How to Read the SHAP Plot

  • Top features have the greatest influence on the model.
  • Red points represent higher feature values.
  • Blue points represent lower feature values.
  • Points on the right increase the probability of a phishing website.
  • Points on the left increase the probability of a legitimate website.

LIME Explanation

LIME (Local Interpretable Model-Agnostic Explanations) explains why the model made a prediction for a single website by highlighting the features that contributed positively or negatively to that prediction.

Feature Importance

The Random Forest model ranks features according to how much they contribute to phishing website detection. Higher importance indicates a greater influence on the model's decisions.

Key Insights

  • Google Index is the most influential feature for identifying legitimate and phishing websites.
  • Page Rank is the second most important feature, indicating that trusted websites generally have higher rankings.
  • Hyperlink-related features such as Number of Hyperlinks, External Hyperlinks, andSafe Anchor play a major role in phishing detection.
  • Domain Age helps distinguish newly created phishing domains from long-established legitimate websites.
  • The Random Forest model achieved an overall Accuracy of 96.02%, demonstrating strong predictive performance.
  • SHAP provides a global explanation of feature importance, while LIME explains individual predictions, making the model more transparent and trustworthy.
